# AI Use Disclosure — Draft

**Status:** Living draft, updated as the pipeline progresses; finalized in
Phase 6 for inclusion in the manuscript's Declarations section. This
document itself is never treated as final until the researcher explicitly
approves the Phase 6 version.

## Draft disclosure text

> During the preparation of this systematic review, the authors used
> Claude Code (Anthropic) as an AI-assisted research and drafting tool for
> the following tasks: (1) drafting the initial PRISMA 2020 protocol
> structure and search query formulations for researcher review; (2)
> constructing and executing reproducible metadata-retrieval queries
> against OpenAlex and Crossref; (3) producing draft title/abstract and
> full-text screening classifications and draft evidence-extraction and
> quality-appraisal records, all of which were reviewed, verified, and
> either approved or overridden by the corresponding author before being
> treated as final; (4) assisting with drafting portions of the manuscript
> text from the human-approved, verified evidence dataset; and (5)
> performing automated integrity checks (DOI format validation, PRISMA
> arithmetic cross-checks, duplicate detection) prior to submission. The
> AI tool did not select final included studies, did not determine venue
> quality classifications, and is not an author. All substantive
> scholarly judgments — eligibility decisions, extraction verification,
> quality appraisal sign-off, taxonomy construction, and interpretation of
> findings — were made by the human author(s), who take full
> responsibility for the accuracy and integrity of the review.

## Principles governing this disclosure

- **No AI authorship.** Consistent with ICMJE-style authorship criteria
  and JAIR's expected norms, no AI tool is listed as an author or
  contributor in the byline.
- **Specificity over vagueness.** The disclosure names concrete pipeline
  stages (query construction, screening drafts, extraction drafts,
  manuscript drafting support, integrity checks) rather than a generic
  "AI was used to assist with this research" statement, so reviewers can
  assess exactly where human judgment was applied.
- **Truthfulness is updated, not assumed.** This draft will be revised at
  Phase 6 to accurately reflect what AI assistance was *actually* used by
  that point (e.g., if certain planned AI-assisted steps were instead done
  manually by the researcher, or vice versa) — it is not treated as a
  boilerplate template to paste in unchanged.
- **Traceable to the audit trail.** Every AI-assisted output referenced in
  this disclosure has a corresponding audit trail in `outputs/logs/` and
  the workbook's AI-draft vs. human-approved columns, so the disclosure's
  claims are independently verifiable from the project's own artifacts.
